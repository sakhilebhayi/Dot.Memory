## 1. What Dot.Memory is

Dot.Memory is the persistence substrate for the Dot Ecosystem: knowledge-graph storage, vector indexes, Knowledge Pack archives, and audit trails that other Dot Ecosystem platforms write to. Dot.Memory is infrastructure, not a knowledge consumer — it stores content without reading it.

## 3. Store without reading

Dot.Memory does not read, model, or act on the content other platforms store with it. This means we can't review, moderate, or vouch for the accuracy of stored content — responsibility for what's stored, and for its lawfulness, rests with the platform that published it.

## 4. Retrieval SLA contracts

Dot.Memory publishes named retrieval-latency contracts for different consumer classes (agent-context, human-facing surfaces, audit, archive), each with a defined on-breach behaviour. These are operational targets, not a guarantee of a specific outcome for any individual request; they're described in full on our public documentation.

## 5. Forgetting policy

We don't delete stored content outright — content that's no longer actively referenced loses salience over time (ageing from Hot to Warm to Cold storage) rather than being erased, preserving a full history and audit trail. Legally required erasure is handled through cryptographic shredding once implemented; see our Privacy Policy for details.
